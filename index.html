About the Program

Computer Engineering at UE

The BS Computer Engineering program at the University of the East trains students to work on both sides of the hardware-software line — from designing circuits to writing the operating systems that run on them. It's a multidisciplinary track built around embedded and computer systems, with a strong focus on hands-on, project-based learning.
