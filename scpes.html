Computer Engineering Students

SCPES is the official student organization of the Computer Engineering program at UE — the home base for every CpE Warrior's academic, professional, and social life on campus.

About SCPES

Who we are

SCPES brings together every CpE student under one organization, giving them a platform to grow technically, take on leadership roles, and build a community outside the classroom. The org works closely with the department to support students throughout their four years in the program.

Organization Objectives

What SCPES stands for

  • ◢Foster camaraderie among CpE students
  • ◢Support academic and technical growth
  • ◢Represent students to the department and university
  • ◢Organize skills-building activities and events
