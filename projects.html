Student
Projects

From semestral requirements to full senior projects, CpE Warriors build across the whole hardware-software spectrum. Here's a look at the categories of work students take on throughout the program.

Project categories

What CpE students design, build, and defend across their four years.

01 / EMBEDDED

Embedded Systems

Microcontroller-based builds — from sensor rigs to custom firmware for dedicated devices.

 02 / IOT

IoT Applications

Connected devices and systems that collect, send, and act on real-world data.

 03 / ROBOTICS

Robotics

Sensor, actuator, and control-system integration for automated and autonomous machines.

 04 / SOFTWARE

Software Systems

Applications, tools, and platforms built to solve real academic or campus problems.

 05 / ML

Machine Learning Projects

Models and intelligent systems applied to classification, prediction, and automation tasks.

 06 / DESIGN

Design Projects

Circuit and system design work grounded in engineering standards and constraints.

 07 / RESEARCH

Research Projects

Original investigations that feed into the department's research and innovation output.
