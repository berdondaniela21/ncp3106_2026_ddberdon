About Computer Engineering

Computer Engineering is a broad field that sits in between the hardware of electrical engineering and the software of computer Science. When compuret engineers design hardware, they focus on what the hardware is trying to accomplish as opposed to the nitty-gritty details of how to lay out the transistors. They design the processors for systems of all sizes, whether they look like computers or not. The processors go into desktop computers, smartphones, tablet computers, supercomputers, and embedded systems. Computer engineers also design the software that runs on these processors, including operating systems, device drivers, and applications. Specialized processors like GPUs (graphics processing units) or hardware accelerate AI algorithms are also designed by computer engineers.

Program Overview

What the program is about

Computer Engineering applies engineering principles to the analysis, design, and integration of hardware and software. UE's CpE curriculum is multidisciplinary by design, preparing graduates to build embedded digital systems — the microprocessor- and microcontroller-based devices found in everything from consumer electronics to industrial automation and gaming hardware. Coursework leans heavily on practical, project-based learning: students design circuits, write firmware, and build systems
