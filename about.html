What is
Computer Engineering?

Computer Engineering is a broad field that sits in between the hardware of Electrical Engineering and the software of Computer Science. When computer engineers design hardware, they focus on what the hardware is trying to accomplish as opposed to the nitty-gritty details of how to lay out the transistors.

They design the processors for systems of all sizes, whether they look like computers or not — desktops, smartphones, tablets, supercomputers, and embedded systems. Computer engineers also design the software that runs on these processors, including operating systems, device drivers, and applications, as well as specialized hardware like GPUs and AI accelerators.

About Computer Engineering

Relationship Between Hardware and Software

Hardware and software have a strictly symbiotic and hierarchical relationship, where hardware provides the physical execution and software provides the logical instructions that configure and drive that machinery. Both are mutually dependent — they must work together to produce a useful output. Hardware refers to the tangible, physical components of a computer system.

About Computer Engineering

Role of Computer Engineers in Modern Technology

Computer Engineering plays an indispensable role in society as technology integrates into every facet of day-to-day life. From smartphones and laptops to smart homes and autonomous vehicles, computer engineers envision, design, and build the systems that power these devices.

They continuously work to enhance existing systems — improving speed, efficiency, and security — as demand for skilled engineers grows alongside the pace of technological advancement. Their expertise spans hardware design, embedded systems, software development, cybersecurity, and network architecture, contributing to industries from healthcare and finance to telecommunications and AI.
